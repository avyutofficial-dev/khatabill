Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to KhataBill ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the KhataBill application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

KhataBill is an offline-first application. By default, all your billing data, customer details, and products are stored locally on your device using IndexedDB.

  • Google Drive Sync: If you choose to enable the Google Drive Sync feature, KhataBill will request permission to access your Google Drive (specifically the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope).
  • We only use this permission to upload and download your own KhataBill backup files (JSON) directly to/from your personal Google Drive account.
  • We do not access, view, or read any other files on your Google Drive.
  • Your data is never uploaded to our servers, and we do not sell or share your data with any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Since the cloud backup relies on Google Drive, your usage of that feature is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your Rights

Because your data lives exclusively on your device and inside your own personal Google Drive, you have complete control over it. You can delete your KhataBill app data at any time via the app settings, or delete the backup file directly from your Google Drive.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our support channels.
