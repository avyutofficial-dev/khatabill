About KhataBill

Your digital billing assistant

KhataBill

Version 1.0.0

KhataBill is a mobile-first, offline-capable digital billing ledger designed specifically for Indian shopkeepers, retailers, and farmers. Manage your transactions, generate PDF invoices, and share bills via WhatsApp instantly—without requiring an internet connection.

Created By AVYUT
Core Features

100% Offline-Ready

All data stays securely on your device. No internet required.

Professional PDF Invoices

Generate clean, beautifully formatted tax invoices to download or print instantly.

WhatsApp Sharing

Send text invoice summaries to your customers on WhatsApp with a single tap.

Privacy Focused

No user tracking, no remote servers, and no ads. Your financial details are strictly private.

Seamless Backup & Restore

Easily download a JSON backup file of all your data and restore it whenever you need to.
